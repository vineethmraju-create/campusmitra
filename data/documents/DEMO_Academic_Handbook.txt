DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — NOT AN OFFICIAL UNIVERSITY POLICY
CampusMitra Sample University Academic Handbook
Version: Sample 2026

ATTENDANCE
Students are expected to maintain at least 75 percent attendance in each course to be eligible for the regular end-semester examination. Attendance is calculated using the classes conducted and classes attended. Students should contact the department office if attendance records appear incorrect. Any approved relaxation must be confirmed by the competent university authority; students should not assume an exception is automatic.

EXAMINATIONS
The end-semester examination timetable is published by the Examination Section through an official notice. Students should check the latest notice for the confirmed examination dates, reporting time, examination hall and permitted materials. The sample handbook does not specify an actual examination start date.

FEES
Tuition and other academic fee payment deadlines are communicated through official fee circulars and the student portal. Students should retain the payment receipt. For payment failures or fee-account discrepancies, contact the Accounts Section with the transaction reference. This sample handbook does not state a real fee amount or deadline.

SCHOLARSHIPS
Eligible students may apply for scholarships announced by government departments or the university. Scholarship eligibility, income limits, required documents and deadlines vary by scheme. Students should verify current details from the official scholarship notification and contact the Student Welfare Office for assistance. This sample handbook does not guarantee a scholarship.

HOSTEL
Hostel accommodation is subject to availability and the hostel admission process. Residents are expected to follow the hostel's published entry timings, visitor rules, cleanliness requirements and safety instructions. Students should contact the Hostel Office for current room availability, charges and rules.

PLACEMENTS
The Placement and Training Cell shares placement opportunities, eligibility criteria, company notices, assessment schedules and interview instructions. Students should read each company notice carefully and register before its stated deadline. Selection criteria are determined by the recruiting organization. Students should contact the Placement Cell for official clarification.

STUDENT SUPPORT
For academic matters, students should first contact their course faculty or department office. For examination matters, contact the Examination Section. For tuition or payment matters, contact the Accounts Section. For hostel matters, contact the Hostel Office. For career and recruitment matters, contact the Placement and Training Cell.
